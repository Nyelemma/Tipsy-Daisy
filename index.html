A little sip of our story

Tipsy Daisy had always been a dream of mine ever since I started working in hospitality. I have worked all over the globe in the industry including being a chef, barista, waitress and events/wedding coordinator.

Tipsy Daisy seemed like the next natural step after working in chalets in the French Alps, weddings in the Lake District and pizza ovens at Glastonbury Festival.

We strive to make every event memorable, whether you need just the bar or so much more. We can help you wet your whistles, line your stomachs and make everything happen for your special occasion
